# ⚽🏈🏀⚾ Super Sports Party!

**Super Sports Party!** is a vibrant, browser-based **4-in-1 sports game** designed specifically for toddlers and young children aged **3–5 years old**. Built with simplicity, positive visual feedback, and zero punishment, this single-file web application offers accessible fun across desktop computers, tablets, and smartphones.

---

## 🎮 Included Mini-Games

1. **⚽ Soccer Goal Striker**
   - **Gameplay**: Aim your shot left or right and kick past the friendly Bear Goalie into the net!
   - **Goal**: Score exciting goals to trigger star celebrations and cheer sound effects.

2. **🏈 Football Touchdown Toss**
   - **Gameplay**: Time your throw to pass the football across the field into the hands of the running Fox Receiver.
   - **Goal**: Complete passes to achieve Touchdowns and earn stars!

3. **🏀 Basketball Tap Tap Shots**
   - **Gameplay**: Arcade *Tap Tap Shots* style floating physics! Tapping the screen or pressing action propels the basketball up and forward towards a moving hoop.
   - **Goal**: Score swishes, bounce off the backboard, and build up streak multipliers to set the ball on fire! 🔥

4. **⚾ Baseball Home Run Hitter**
   - **Gameplay**: Single-tap timing swing! A friendly pitcher throws a big slow ball to home plate. Time your swing to blast the ball out of the field!
   - **Goal**: Hit big Home Runs to unleash fireworks, star rewards, and cheers!

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Toddler-Centric Design (Ages 3–5)**:
  - **Zero Fail States**: Missing a shot simply resets the ball gently with positive audio cues—children never lose their overall score or encounter a "Game Over" screen.
  - **High Success Rates**: Forgiving collision boundaries and generous hitboxes.
  - **Vibrant Visuals**: Powered by Tailwind CSS and HTML5 Canvas with glowing star counters, confetti particle explosions, and cheering banners.

- **Dual Control System**:
  - **Touch Screen Ready**: Massive, easy-to-tap buttons designed for small hands on tablets and mobile devices.
  - **Keyboard Friendly**: Full support for physical key bindings.

- **Self-Contained Web Audio API Engine**:
  - All sound effects (whistles, kicks, bounce sounds, net swishes, bat hits, cheers, and horns) are generated dynamically using browser sound synthesis. No external `.mp3` or `.wav` files required.

## 🕹️ Controls Guide

| Action | Keyboard | Touch Screen / Mouse |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Action** *(Kick / Throw / Shoot / Swing)* | `Spacebar`, `Enter`, or `Up Arrow` | Tap Giant Center **"KICK! / THROW! / SHOOT! / SWING!"** Button OR tap directly on the Game Canvas |
| **Aim / Move Left** | `Left Arrow` or `A` | Tap Big **⬅️ Left Arrow** Button |
| **Aim / Move Right** | `Right Arrow` or `D` | Tap Big **➡️ Right Arrow** Button |
| **Toggle Sound** | Click Sound Button in top bar | Tap **🔊 Sound ON/OFF** Button |
| **Return to Menu** | Click Menu Button in top bar | Tap **🏠 Menu** Button |
